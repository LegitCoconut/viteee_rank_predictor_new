# VITEEE 2027: The Complete Student's Guide

Nov 14, 2026

VITEEE 2027 is a 2-hour, 100-question online test that decides B.Tech admission at all four VIT campuses, and applications close on 31 March 2027.

## What is VITEEE?

VITEEE (VIT Engineering Entrance Examination) is the only route into B.Tech at Vellore Institute of Technology. Admission is rank-based: your VITEEE rank, eligibility and seat availability decide where you land. JEE Main and SAT scores are not considered.

One exam covers four campuses: VIT Vellore, VIT Chennai, VIT-AP and VIT Bhopal. You choose campus, programme and fee category later, during counselling, not on the application form.

You can appear only once per cycle, so the single attempt matters.

## Eligibility

You qualify if you are a 2025 or 2026 Class XII pass-out, or appearing in 2027, with at least 60% in your core subjects.

- **Nationality:** Indian citizens, NRIs, or OCI cardholders with an Indian residential address. Holders of only a foreign passport are not eligible through this form.
- **Qualifying exam:** regular, full-time, formal-mode Class XII from a single board, with English as a subject.
- **Marks (general):** 60% aggregate in Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics (or Biology), with at least 50% in Mathematics or Biology.
- **Marks (SC/ST, J&K, Ladakh, northeastern states):** 50% aggregate in the same subjects.
- **Age limit:** none stated.
- **Biology stream:** PCB candidates are eligible only for specific programmes such as Biotechnology, Bioinformatics, Health Sciences and Technology, and Bioengineering.

VIT verifies eligibility after tuition fee payment, and you must bring documents when reporting.

## Exam pattern and marking

The paper is a computer-based test: 100 multiple-choice questions in 2 hours, English only, with a separate time limit for each section.

| Section | Questions | Time limit | Time per question\* |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mathematics or Biology | 40 | 50 min | 75 sec |
| Physics | 25 | 30 min | 72 sec |
| Chemistry | 25 | 30 min | 72 sec |
| English and Aptitude | 10 (5 + 5) | 10 min | 60 sec |

\*Our arithmetic from the official limits, not an official figure.

Scoring is +4 for a correct answer, -1 for a wrong one, and 0 for unanswered or marked-for-review questions. That makes the maximum 400 marks (100 x 4).

**What this means for preparation (our analysis):**

- Mathematics or Biology carries 40% of the paper. It is the largest single lever on your rank.
- A blind guess has a positive expected value only if your chance of being right is above 20%, since you gain 4 or lose 1. Guess when you can eliminate options, skip when you cannot.
- Questions marked for review score zero. Answer them before you leave the section, or you forfeit the marks.
- English and Aptitude is only 10 questions, but 40 marks is the same as 10 Physics questions. Do not treat it as filler.
- Practise with a timer per section, not one 2-hour block, to match how the real test is paced.

## Fee, counselling and admission rules

The application fee is Rs. 1,450 for centres in India, or the equivalent of USD 90 in INR for centres abroad. It is non-refundable and, per the official page, covers the application and subsequent counselling.

- Seats go by rank. There is no management quota.
- Counselling is online, and you choose campus, programme and fee category there.
- After allotment, changes of branch, campus or category are not permitted. Decide your preferences carefully before you lock in.
- Helpline: 044-46277555, Monday to Saturday, 9:00 am to 5:00 pm, excluding public holidays. After logging in, use the "Need Help" menu for application queries.

## Mistakes to avoid

1. **Trusting third-party pattern tables.** At least one coaching site lists a 25-25-25 split for the three main subjects. The official page shows Mathematics or Biology at 40, Physics at 25, Chemistry at 25, plus 5 English and 5 Aptitude. Always cross-check against viteee.vit.ac.in.
2. **Assuming dates are final.** Every date here is tentative, and third-party sites disagree (one expected registration on 24 October, the official site says 20 October). Use the official portal.
3. **Leaving questions on mark-for-review.** They score zero, same as unanswered.
4. **Ignoring the eligibility floor.** 60% aggregate and 50% in Mathematics or Biology is checked after you pay tuition, so a shortfall found late is costly.
5. **Treating the branch choice as reversible.** It is not: no changes of branch, campus or category after allotment.
6. **Planning around a second attempt.** Candidates can appear only once.
